How to install on Android

Set up on iPhone
  1. Open in Safari

    Open this website in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.

  2. Tap Share

    Tap the square with an upward arrow, then choose Add to Home Screen. Share may be inside the More menu.

  3. Add KU Bus

    If shown, leave Open as Web App switched on, then tap Add. Open KU Bus from its new Home Screen icon.
